# Lesson template

Copy this shape exactly. Write the section headings in the learner's language: the Chinese headings are shown in brackets. Keep the frontmatter keys in English.

```markdown
---
day: 7
date: 2026-12-01
title: "DPO：不用 RL 的偏好优化"
stage: "RL 与偏好对齐"
summary: "One sentence: the core takeaway, same as the TL;DR line."
---

## TL;DR  [一句话]

The single most important takeaway, in one sentence.

## The idea  [讲清楚]

3–5 sentences: what it is, how it works, why it was invented, where it sits in the training pipeline.

## Analogy  [类比]

One everyday analogy that maps onto the mechanism.

## Why it matters  [和数据业务的关系]

What this means for the learner's work (from learner.md). For data-industry learners: what data this method consumes, who buys it, and what a vendor must get right (format, quality, volume).

## Say it like a pro  [客户沟通用语]

> *One natural English sentence the learner can use with researchers or clients.*

## Terms  [术语]

| English | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) | 直接偏好优化 |
| Preference pair | 偏好对 |

## Sources  [来源]

- [Paper or page title](https://example.com) — what it was used for
```

Rules:

- `summary` = the TL;DR sentence, plain text, no markdown.
- `stage` = the `## ` heading of the curriculum stage the topic came from.
- 3–6 terms. 2–4 sources, only pages you actually opened.
